# M13. Maps reviewed

REQUIREMENTS U10, U8, U7. Comparing, deciding, and commenting from the field, on maps.

- Comparing two versions of a map (M8), by a swipe across it or side by side: features added, removed, and moved,
  each lit on the map and listed in the margin's changes.
- A decision's option may point at a place (M12): hovering or focusing it lights the place on the map, and its link
  pans and zooms the map to it, as an option's passage does (U7).
- On a phone, a comment where the reader stands: Louvre's page asks for the browser's location on a tap, never the
  artifact's frame, and opens a point's thread there with the reading's accuracy.

Accept: on a phone and a laptop, the operator compares two versions of a map and finds a moved feature, answers a
decision whose options are places, and, on a phone outdoors, comments where they stand.
