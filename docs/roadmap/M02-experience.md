# M2. Reading and commenting, over mock data

REQUIREMENTS U1, U2, U3, U6, L2, L6, L7. The experience in `ui/`, on canopy's web stack and its strictness, served by
a FastAPI app over mock data, as canopy's demo is. The store comes after it and plugs in under it (M4).

- An artifact's page: its versions, its HTML rendered on an origin of its own in a sandboxed frame, and its threads in
  the margin beside their passages, following the page as it scrolls.
- Selecting words to open a thread, replying, editing and deleting one's comment, and resolving and reopening a
  thread; a thread whose passage left a later version shown as `passage_gone`, never moved to other words.
- An agent's comment marked as an agent's, on behalf of the person who minted its token, apart from a person's.
- A designed HTML report, scripts and all, rendered as its author made it (U3), dark first (U2).
- A list of artifacts to reach them from, filtered by origin.
- Louvre's style as a versioned stylesheet and the rules for its components, from the chosen direction (U6).
- Iterated with the operator on the running page until it feels right.

Accept: the operator, on the running page, reads an artifact, comments on a passage, replies, and resolves a thread,
across a new version, and says it is right; Playwright holds each of those interactions.

Status: not started.
