Checkout latency: what we found and what to change

Since the 14 October release, p95 checkout latency rose from 410 ms to 1.2 s, and carts abandoned at the payment step rose by 6%. This report traces where the time goes and proposes three changes, smallest first.

What we measured

We replayed a day of production traffic against staging with tracing on, and read the spans of 40,000 checkouts.

Regionp50p95p95 before
us-east290 ms940 ms400 ms
eu-west350 ms1,610 ms430 ms

Where the time goes

Two thirds of the added time is the tax quote. The new tax service is called once per line item instead of once per cart, and each call waits on a cold connection pool. The payment provider's own p95 held at 180 ms, so the provider is not the cause.

The rest is the fraud check, which now runs before the payment form renders instead of after the customer submits it.

Proposal

  1. Batch the tax quote into one call per cart. This alone should bring p95 under 700 ms.
  2. Warm the tax service's connection pool at deploy, and cache the tax quote per cart for 60 seconds.
  3. Move the fraud check back to submit time, where it ran before the release.

Risks

A cached quote can show a stale price if a promotion starts mid-session. Moving the fraud check delays a decline until submit, which the old flow already did.
