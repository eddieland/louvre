Why it feels heavier

1

Nothing leads

Every block is a panel with the same hairline and uppercase label, so a worker’s question and a session id get the same weight.

2

The nav follows the journal, not your day

Runs, ticks, worktrees, chores, spend and memory are each a section because each is a table. You work in tickets.

3

The thing you do is small

Answering is a text box in a corner. Reading a ticket means stitching Conversation, Runs and Decisions back into one story.

4

Color is spent on identity

Eleven section hues, an aurora and the ticker’s flair compete with the five status colors that carry meaning.

5

The rules grew one incident at a time

Most of the 1,339 lines are right. Needing that many is a sign the layout fights what it holds, and every new feature gets a panel because a panel is the only shape on offer.
