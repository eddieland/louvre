Keeps

  • IBM Plex, the indigo slate, every status token and its contrast tests
  • The counters and the list beside the thread
  • Monospace readouts for every number

Cuts

  • The rail: search moves to the header, Working moves under the list, spend becomes a counter
  • Section colors, the aurora, the ticker, odometers
  • Uppercase panel labels on every block; a page has one card per idea

Costs

Small to medium. Tokens and type stay. The shell and the nav change; seven sections move inside others.

Risk

It might simply grow back. Nothing in the design stops the next panel.

Is it E you stopped liking, or what grew on top of it?
