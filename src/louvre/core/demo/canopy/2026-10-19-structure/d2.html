Direction 2 · a mail client for workers

Mailroom

Round one’s calm inbox taken all the way. Three panes: mailboxes, messages, the open thread with its composer. Runs, ticks and notes become small lines inside the thread, so a ticket reads as one story. The machinery lives in a folder you rarely open.

Keeps

  • The thread as chat bubbles with a reply box
  • Status as dots and chips; the accent for the action
  • Every write still a CLI verb, with its refusal

Cuts

  • Pages per table: Ticks, Worktrees, Memory and Settings fold into one line at the foot
  • Separate Decisions, Runs and Conversation panels on a ticket
  • Section colors, aurora, ticker, panel labels

Costs

Medium. A new shell and a new ticket page that merges three panels into one timeline. Lists keep their queries.

Risk

A mail client invites you to clear it. If the list rarely reaches zero, it nags instead of calms.

Should a ticket be a conversation first and a record second?
