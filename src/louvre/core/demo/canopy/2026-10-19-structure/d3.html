Ledger

Round two’s F, revived without the keyboard requirement. All monospace, a status line in place of a header, bracketed tags, and a ticket as a single time-ordered log. One color means “you”: amber marks what waits on you and the prompt you answer at.

Keeps

  • The density you get from mono and hairlines
  • The journal as the truth: the log is the journal, in order
  • State colors, held to the same contrast rules

Cuts

  • Panels altogether: sections are rules of text, not boxes
  • Ticks, chores and decisions as pages: one “log” tab streams them
  • Bubbles, odometers, section colors, the rail

Costs

Medium. A new type system and tone; the lists and data stay. Easy to keep simple, because there is so little chrome to add to.

Risk

Agent prose in mono is tiring to read at length, and that is most of what you read.

Would you rather canopy look like the terminal it came from?
