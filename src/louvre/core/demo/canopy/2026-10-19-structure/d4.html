Direction 4 · a page that tells you

Briefing

Round one’s field notes, kept for structure rather than skin. Today is one page: a headline that counts what needs you, your answer first, then what broke, then what waits on others. A ticket is a story with the facts in the margin. Workers’ words are set in a reading face, because reading them is the job.

Keeps

  • The list and detail split: the answer box sits in the page
  • Every section of the nav but Today, Tickets and Log
  • Counters as tiles: the numbers are in the sentence

Costs

Large. A new page model, a headline written from the attention list, and new type.

Risk

It reads beautifully with six items and could sprawl with thirty. The headline has to stay true and short.
