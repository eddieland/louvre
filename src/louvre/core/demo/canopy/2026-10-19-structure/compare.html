Questions that decide it

  • Is the problem the look or the amount? If direction 1 feels right, it was the amount, and the fix is a cull rather than a redesign.
  • Thread or record? Directions 2 and 4 make a ticket one story. Today and direction 1 keep facts beside it.
  • How often do you open Ticks, Worktrees, Chores or Memory? Weekly or less, and they can leave the nav in any direction.
  • Reading face? Most of what you read is a worker’s prose. Direction 4 is the only one that sets it for reading.
  • Still dark first? Every drawing has both themes; the toggle sits above it.
