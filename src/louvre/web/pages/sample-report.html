Report · PAY-142

Checkout latency: what we found and what to change

Since the 14 October release, p95 checkout latency rose from 410 ms to 1.2 s, and carts abandoned at the payment step rose by 6%. Two changes in the release explain nearly all of it, and both are cheap to undo.

Where the time goes

The added time at p95, split by what it was spent waiting on.

The new tax service is called once per line item instead of once per cart, and each call waits on a cold connection pool. The fraud check now runs before the payment form renders, instead of after the customer submits it.

The payment provider's own p95 held at 180 ms throughout: the provider is not the cause.

Proposal

  1. Batch the tax quote into one call per cart Alone, brings p95 under 700 ms. A day's work.
  2. Warm the pool at deploy, and cache the quote per cart for 60 s Removes the cold start; the cache needs the promotion's version in its key.
  3. Move the fraud check back to submit time Where it ran before the release; declines arrive at submit, as they used to.
